60-0 Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

60-0 is a single-player game made by hephaestuz, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app keeps on your phone, what leaves it, and who receives it. In short: the developer collects nothing. Ads are handled by Google.

What the app stores

The app keeps your game on your phone: the campaign in progress, recent results, best records, Daily Campaign results and streak, achievements, the commanders you have drafted, skip counts and the last mode you played. None of it is sent to the developer. Google’s ads software also keeps your ad consent choices and its own working data on the phone.

The app keeps this data out of cloud backups: on Android, app backup is turned off, and on iOS the file is excluded from iCloud backup. It is not copied to your Google or Apple account backup or moved to a new phone.

No accounts

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal details, and it shows no permission prompts. The app itself asks for no permissions. On Android, the permissions it holds come from Google’s ads software: network access, the advertising ID, and the background-work permissions that software needs. None of them prompts you. On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer runs no servers for this app, and the app contains no analytics, tracking or crash reporting of its own.

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google or Apple in your device settings, Google Play or App Store Connect may show the developer anonymous crash and performance reports. That sharing is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one: a rewarded video that gives a re-spin once a skip type has run out. There are no other ads.

To show and measure ads, Google’s ads software in the app collects information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app details, and your interactions with ads. Google uses it under its own privacy policy, including to show personalized ads where you have allowed them.

Where the law requires it, the app asks for your choices through Google’s consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. When that applies to you, you can change your choices at any time under About, Privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit personalized ads in your device settings, usually under Privacy, Ads or Google, Ads. Google ad settings: https://myadcenter.google.com. Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Fonts

On Android, the app’s display font is downloaded from Google Fonts through Google Play services, and Google may log that request; if the font cannot be downloaded, a built-in font is used. On iOS the display font is built into the app and nothing is downloaded.

Sharing

Share sends a short text, and usually an image of your result or of a commander card, to the app you pick in your phone’s share menu. Nothing leaves your phone unless you pick a share target, and what that app does with it is covered by its own policy. The image is kept in the app’s cache on your phone.

Children

60-0 is not directed at children under 13. The developer collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the app stores, uninstall it. On Android you can also clear its storage in Settings, Apps, 60-0, Storage. Data Google holds for ads is managed through your device and Google ad settings above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on its web page with a new effective date.

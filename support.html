60-0 Support

Help with 60-0, a single-player game for iOS and Android made by hephaestuz, an independent developer.

Contact

Email hephaestuz76@gmail.com with questions, problems or feedback. Please include your phone model, its iOS or Android version, and the version shown at the top of About in the app.

Questions

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game stores is on your phone, and the developer holds nothing about you. On iPhone, delete the app. On Android, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Settings, Apps, 60-0, Storage. The game is not kept in any backup, so deleting it is permanent.

When does the Daily Campaign reset?

At midnight on your phone’s clock, in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same five opening spins for the same date, on iOS and Android, as long as both have the latest update. You get one attempt a day, with no skips. Finish a Daily on consecutive days to build your streak; miss a day and it starts again.

How do Challenge codes work?

When you share a Classic or Challenge campaign, the message includes an 8-character code. A friend taps Enter code on the Spin screen, types or pastes the code, and starts from the same five opening spins you had. The same skip at the same point gives both of you the same new spin. Codes work between iOS and Android.

What does “made on a different version” mean?

Each code carries the roster version of the app that made it. When an update changes the roster of commanders, the same code would deal different spins, so the app refuses codes from another roster. Make sure you and your friend both have the latest update. If the code was made before the update, ask for a new one from a campaign played after it.

Why are there no ads on iOS?

The iOS app has no ads in this version, so it offers no ad re-spins: each campaign has its free skips and your banked skips. Ads may come to iOS in a later update; if they do, the privacy policy will change first.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, and Android 7.1 or later.

Privacy

The developer collects nothing. The 60-0 privacy policy explains what each app keeps on your phone and what leaves it, with a part for the iOS app and a part for the Android app.
